I am a Researcher (formerly Junior Group Leader) at the [Institute for Interdisciplinary Mountain Research](https://www.oeaw.ac.at/en/igf/home), part of the [Austrian Academy of Sciences](https://www.oeaw.ac.at/igf) in Innsbruck, Austria. Previously, I was an Assistant Professor in Geo‑Environmental Data Science at [Utrecht University](https://www.uu.nl/staff/MGravey) and a Postdoctoral Fellow at [Stanford University](https://scerf.stanford.edu/). My PhD from the [University of Lausanne](https://unil.ch/gaia) focused on geostatistics — still a core pillar of my work.

## Focus Areas

### Computer Science
- Machine Learning
- High‑Performance Computing (HPC)

### Remote Sensing
- GIS
- Google Earth Engine (Google Developer Expert)

### Geostatistics
- Multiple‑Point Statistics (MPS)

## Current Work
I’m developing a passive sensing approach using photogrammetry to generate digital elevation models (DEM) from a single flyover. I also collaborate on projects spanning remote sensing, geostatistics, and bird tracking.

If you’re a student, see [Advices](/advices/) and [4Students](/4students/).
